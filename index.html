My decisions

Manual decisions and notes only. They are saved in this browser/device, not synced to Oriel or backed up elsewhere. Download backup creates the file that can be restored later; Export decisions creates a readable CSV and cannot restore your working state. Imported Oriel preferences remain visible in the explorer but are not added here unless you change them.

Privacy & data

Independent tool. Oriel Placement Explorer is not affiliated with Oriel or NHS England. Choices and notes made here are not submitted back to Oriel.

What stays on this device

Your Oriel spreadsheet is parsed in your browser. The loaded programme data, manual decisions, notes, map-location cache, preferences and your most recent location search are stored in this browser only. There is no Oriel Placement Explorer server receiving your spreadsheet.

External services

  • Postcodes.io receives programme postcodes so they can be placed on the map. If you search using a postcode, that postcode is also sent to Postcodes.io.
  • Nominatim / OpenStreetMap receives place-name searches such as a town or city.
  • Esri supplies the map tiles displayed in the explorer.
  • Google Maps is opened only when you click Directions; the destination postcode and, if set, your chosen origin are included in that link.
  • cdnjs / Cloudflare supplies the Leaflet mapping library.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and, like normal web hosting, may process technical request information such as IP address for delivery and security.

If you prefer not to use your full home postcode for distance searching, use an outward postcode (for example HA9) or a nearby town instead.

Usage analytics

If configured, GoatCounter is used only for basic visit/action counts such as a page visit, successful spreadsheet load or decisions export. The explorer does not send spreadsheet contents, programme names, postcodes, preference ranks, notes or Yes/No decisions to analytics. You can turn analytics off here at any time.

Saving and backups

Your work can disappear if browser/site data is cleared or you switch browser/device. Use Download backup after a session if you want a restorable copy. Export decisions is useful as a readable CSV, but it is not a restore file.

Clearing saved data removes the loaded spreadsheet cache, decisions, notes, cached map locations and explorer settings from this browser. Your analytics opt-out preference is kept.
